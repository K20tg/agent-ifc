Whether the model falls for it is basically luck. So the guard doesn't try to read minds. Everything the agent touches gets a band for where it came from: orange for the open web, blue for private files. At the exit, one dumb rule: orange and blue together don't leave. The rule looks at the bands, not the words, so there's nothing to sweet-talk.
